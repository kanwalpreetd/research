# Research at the Stellar Development Foundation

The [Stellar Development Foundation](https://stellar.org) supports technological and scientific research through its in-house research team and its academic grants program.

Heads up! We are looking for a [Director of Research](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/stellar/4cada7d9-0387-45d4-8965-6783690cf720).

We are also looking for a research intern in formal methods to explore methods to formalize and reason about production systems, in the vein of our [CAV 2026](https://conferences.i-cav.org/2026/) paper ["Show Me The Money: An Exercise in Proof-Driven Software Understanding"](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-32537-2_11).
See the [internships page](/internships) for details on our internship program.
